FAQ

What to know before you ride

What can I do with Napigation?
Set a proximity alert for a stop or destination, including for a saved route. You can customize alert sound, volume, and vibration, and use the app without an account or ads. Napigation is a stop reminder, not turn-by-turn navigation.
What is free, and what does Premium add?
The core alert experience does not require a subscription. The free plan supports up to five saved locations and one route. Premium adds unlimited saved locations and routes, one-time destinations, external navigation, and additional alert controls. Subscription and one-time purchase options and their prices are shown in the app.
How does Napigation handle location and other data?
GPS for an active alert is processed on your device, and saved locations and routes stay on your device. Pseudonymous usage and technical reports may be sent to service providers; address and map-point lookups may share information with external geocoding providers. We do not store raw GPS coordinates or saved destination details on our servers. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
Will alerts work with the screen off or phone on silent?
The app is designed to continue monitoring in the background. Background alerts depend on location and notification permissions, device settings, and battery-management behavior. On supported devices, Napigation can make alerts more noticeable; keep media volume above zero.
