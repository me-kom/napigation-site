How it works

Three simple steps

1

Choose your stop

Search for an address or pick a point on the map, then choose when you want to receive the alert before arrival.

2

Turn on the alert

When the trip begins, enable the alert. Napigation keeps tracking even when your phone is in your pocket or the screen is off.

Ride without watching the screen

FAQ

What to know before you start

How do I get started with Napigation?
Download the app, choose a destination, turn on an alert, and continue your trip. You will be notified when your stop is close.
How does Napigation differ from standard GPS navigation apps?
Regular navigation apps are made for constant guidance with the screen in use. Napigation is for riders who only need a reliable reminder before getting off.
Can I use Napigation without paying?
Yes. The app is free to download and includes the core daily-use experience. Advanced capabilities are available for riders who want expanded functionality.
Will the app keep working if my phone is in my pocket with the screen off?
That is the point of the app. Napigation is designed to keep working in the background so you do not need to watch the screen during the ride.
Who is Napigation for?
Napigation is built for bus, train, and public transport riders who want to read, work, or rest during a trip and receive an alert before they need to get off.
What is your data collection policy?
Your GPS location is processed on-device and never sent to our servers or stored, and no account is required. To improve the app we collect only anonymous, aggregated analytics with no personal identification, and address searches are forwarded to an external geocoding service.
Will my phone ring if it is set to Silent or Vibrate?
On supported devices, the app attempts to make the alert noticeable even when the phone is on silent or vibrate. It is still a good idea to make sure media volume is not set to zero.
Is there an iPhone (iOS) version?
The Android app is available now on Google Play. The iOS version is in active development and beta testing, and will be released soon on the App Store.
