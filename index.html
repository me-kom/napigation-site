How does Napigation work?

Choose a destination, start the alert, and let Napigation monitor the ride in the background until your stop is close.

Why is Napigation different from standard GPS navigation apps?

Navigation apps are built for constant screen-led guidance. Napigation is for passengers who just need a reliable reminder before they need to get off.

Can I use Napigation for free?

Yes. The base plan covers the core daily-use experience, while premium plans add more saved destinations, routes, and advanced controls.

Is my location data private?

Yes. Sensitive location processing is handled on-device, no sign-up is required, and private trip location data is not stored in Napigation servers.

Is there an iPhone version?

The Android app is live now on Google Play. The iPhone version is in progress and will be announced when it is ready for the App Store.
